05 / Technical depth

Keep the mathematics close.

The output adds the weighted contributions from the three values.

Open the equation and symbol guide
y=∑i∈{A,B,C}wivi
0.50 × 2 + 0.30 × 8 + 0.20 × 5 = 4.40
w
A nonnegative share; the three shares add to one.
v
A value being combined. Here: 2, 8, and 5.
y
The resulting weighted mixture.

This isolates a weighted-combination step. It does not simulate how an attention model derives its weights.
